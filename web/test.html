How Image to Text Converter works

  
    
    
  1. Open a picture, GIF or video: Open file…, drag it onto the page, or paste with Ctrl+V.
    2. 
    
  2. Pick a look in the Look tab. Every thumbnail is your own image in that style.
    3. 
    
  3. Adjust size, light and outlines in Adjust; colours in Colour; framing in Crop.
    4. 
    
  4. Share or save on the right: send to a chat app as text, or save a PNG / GIF / MP4.
    5. 
  

  
Looking for something? Press Ctrl+K (or 🔍 at the top) and type what you want to do.

  
Good to know

  
    
    
  • Outlines keep shapes recognisable, especially when small. Try the Comic or Line art looks.
    • 
    
  • Chat apps are narrow on phones. Use Send in a chat app → Fix all and calibrate your phone once.
    • 
    
  • Original colours are for pictures and videos. App colours show only the colours a chat app can display in text.
    • 
    
  • Advanced (top right) shows every expert setting.
    • 
    
  • GIFs and videos get a player with a filmstrip; set Start here / End here to export only part.
    • 
  

  
Tools

  
    
    
  • Point at a look to see it on your picture before you pick it; Bigger previews shows them all large.
    • 
    
  • Wipe (above the picture): drag the divider to compare the original and the result.
    • 
    
  • Crop → Draw the crop on the picture: drag a box; drag its edges or corners to change it.
    • 
    
  • ✎ Edit: click or drag on the result to put any character there (type a key to choose it). Edits stay in every export.
    • 
    
  • Caption (Look tab): words on the picture, as plain characters or big letters in the style itself.
    • 
    
  • Animate this picture (Look tab, for still pictures): typewriter, Matrix rain, glitch, scanlines or slow zoom, saved as GIF / MP4 / WEBP.
    • 
    
  • Emoji and App & retro colours (Game Boy, C64, amber…) are in the Look and Colour tabs. Coloured ANSI art (.ans) opens like any file.
    • 
    
  • On a phone: the buttons at the bottom open the settings; pinch to zoom, swipe between Result and Original.
    • 
  

  
Continue later

  
Your last picture and everything you did to it is kept in this browser (on this computer only), so the start screen can offer to continue. More → Forget my last picture removes it.

  
Projects

  
More → Save project (Ctrl+S) keeps the picture with every setting, the crop, mask, hand edits and captions in one .textart file. Open it with Open file… (or drop it here) to continue later, or send it to someone.

Text → Image

  
Paste ASCII, braille or block text art, or coloured ANSI art (terminal escape codes, .ans files). It is drawn with the current font, colour and size settings and can be exported as PNG, JPG, WEBP, SVG or HTML.

  
  

    

    

    

    

    

    

    

  

  

    
    
    
    
  



  
Calibrate for your phone

  
Phones draw braille with different fonts, widths and line spacing. This one-minute check measures yours for Discord, so pasted art keeps its width and shape. It's saved for this platform.

  
    
    
  1. Copy the test card and send it to yourself in Discord (a private chat, "Saved messages" or a test server).
    2. 
    
  2. On your phone, find the longest bar that stays on one line (its length is written at the start).
    3. 
    
  3. Which ring looks most like a circle? (not tall, not flat)
      
    
        
        
      
    4. 
  

  



  
Save or share a look

  
A look is the style, colours, light and line settings (not the picture, width, crop or export options). Saved looks are kept in this browser; a link or a .textlook file gives the look to someone else.
