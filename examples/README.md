# Examples

The `…-Normal` pictures are the sources; the other picture with the same first name is one look
applied by Image to Text Converter (Pixel, Dot, Letter, Emoji — as in the file names). `Standing-Text.txt`
is the standing character (`Standing-ImageNormal.png`) adapted for Discord: paste it into a chat
and it shows as braille dots. `app-screenshot.png` is the app itself — the look cards always
preview your own picture.

The `Video…` GIFs are 7-second reduced previews of two video conversions (Dots and Quadrant
mosaic); the full-quality originals are attached to the `desktop-v1.0.0` release.

The sources:
- The character illustrations are by Zeronoid, the project’s author (same MIT license as the repository).
- `astronaut.jpg` — Bruce McCandless II during the first untethered spacewalk, 1984.
  NASA photograph, public domain (via Wikimedia Commons).

To recreate the results: open the app, open a source picture, pick a look (for pictures on a
plain or transparent background, raise "Hide plain background" in the Adjust tab), and use
Share / save — or "Send in a chat app" for a chat-ready text copy.
